design(review-recovery): repair design for Qwen merged-review reliability

Design-only artifact (no code change): maps a facet-scoped recovery
dispatcher onto execute_merged_readme_review that detects truncation,
malformed arguments, top-level schema failure, transport failure, and
per-facet grounding failure around the single existing merged-review
call, and recovers only the unresolved facet(s) through the existing
blind/factual separated clients -- most notably wiring the factual
fallback client that separated_readme_review.py already constructs and
currently discards.

Bounded to 1 normal call / 5 worst-case physical calls via
max_attempts_override=2 on every recovery-path grounding retry. Adds a
typed QwenReviewRecoveryReceiptV1 for observability; zero-call no-op
reuse (AGENT_APPROVED -> NO_OP_PROVEN) and the MAX_REPAIR_ATTEMPTS=2
repair-loop cap are both preserved unchanged.

See runs/owner_audit_staging/qwen-review-recovery-aa9981021/REPORT.md
for the full design, gap analysis, and requirements traceability.
